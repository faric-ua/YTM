# YTM Importer v1.4.22 — Visual Structure Polish

## Goal

Move the real Home screen closer to the approved three-theme concept without changing playlist/auth/search/write behavior.

## Visual changes

- real vector icons replace Unicode pseudo-icons on Home actions;
- a compact theme-accent music logo appears in the header;
- decorative contours are reduced to two subtle strokes;
- READY and ATTENTION workflow steps keep dark surfaces and use semantic icon/outline color;
- REQUIRED can remain the stronger call-to-action;
- current playlist summary/status is presented as a dedicated card;
- Neon Dark, Blue Dark and Green Dark remain the same three theme families.

## Why

v1.4.21 proved that the theme engine works on a real phone, but its first visual pass was too busy:
- too many contour strokes;
- inconsistent glyph icons;
- solid green READY buttons dominated the screen.

v1.4.22 is the first polish pass toward the approved concept.
