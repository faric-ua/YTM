### EVIDENCE_01 — Home before, v1.4.24 Green

Purpose: historical before-state for the Accent Card System.

The workflow card already had contour strokes, while the current-playlist and track cards were less consistently accented.

### EVIDENCE_02 — Home v1.4.25 Blue

Proves:

- installed version is v1.4.25;
- current-playlist card uses accent strokes;
- main track cards use accent strokes;

Proves:

Proves:

- old hard-coded red selection tint is removed;

### EVIDENCE_06 — History v1.4.25 Blue

Proves:
